This bridge follows chapter updates of a work on Author.Today. It uses the site's own reader API, so no browser or proxy is required.

  • Two modes, never mixed. Either chapter previews (cover and work status) or full chapter text — nothing else.

  • Access follows your session. Anonymously you get free chapters. With account cookies you also get everything your account can already read, including purchased chapters. Nothing is bypassed — the site decides what to serve.

  • Early-access friendly. Chapters that are still closed are skipped, and the next readable ones are collected instead. They are re-checked once a day.

  • Readable in any theme. Author-specified text colours are stripped, images become responsive, links are absolutised.

Configuration

Configuration is optional. Anonymous reading works out of the box; account cookies only add content your account already has access to.

  1. Log in to author.today in your browser.

  2. Open developer tools (F12) → Storage → Cookies (Firefox) or Application → Cookies (Chrome) → https://author.today.

  3. Copy the values of LoginCookie, TrustedToken and ngLoginCookie, then add them to your /config/.bridges file:

    [AuthorTodayBridge]
login_cookie = "your-LoginCookie-value"
trusted_token = "your-TrustedToken-value"
ng_login_cookie = "your-ngLoginCookie-value"

  4. Restart your RSS-Bridge container to apply the configuration:

    docker restart rss-bridge

💡 Note: Your account ID is detected automatically — you do not need to configure it. The optional user_id setting exists only as a fallback if detection ever fails; a wrong value breaks decoding, so leave it out unless the log asks for it.

⚠️ Security: These cookies are keys to your account. Never share them or paste them into public issue reports. They are stored only in your local /config/.bridges and sent only to author.today. Cookie values are never written to the log.

Bridge parameters

  • Story ID: Numeric work ID (e.g. 230933) or a URL — both /work/230933 and /reader/230933/6583841 forms are accepted.

  • Disable tags: Do not attach the work's tags as item categories (enabled by default).

  • Fetch full chapters: Deliver full chapter text instead of previews. The feed then contains only readable chapters — no covers, no statistics, no preview padding.

  • Chapters with full text: How many chapters to deliver (default and maximum: 3). Closed chapters do not count towards this limit.

Caching

The feed itself is cached for 15 minutes. Chapter text is kept for 7 days, since published chapters do not change. Chapters that were closed are re-checked after 1 day — so a chapter you have just bought, or one that opened for free, appears within a day. Restart the container if you want it immediately.

Troubleshooting

Check which access mode actually worked with docker logs rss-bridge | grep AuthorTodayBridge:

  • decoded with the account key — your cookies work, account access is active.

  • fell back to the anonymous key — the session has expired; copy fresh cookies from the browser.

  • No freely readable chapters found — every recent chapter is closed for your session; they will be re-checked after a day.

💡 Tip: Chapters served from the cache are logged at DEBUG level only, so a repeat generation shows no new access check. Set LOG_LEVEL=debug for the full picture.
